## tax_ltcg_rate

**Result:** 12.5%

12.5% base rate within these provisions, not a universal rate for every receipt. Qualifying equity gains under section 198 use the aggregate annual threshold. ## cap_ulip_tax_free_2_5l

**Result:** ₹2.5 Lakh

Aggregate annual premium threshold for relevant ULIPs issued on or after 1 February 2021. The threshold alone does not establish exemption. Apply aggregation across relevant policies and premium years, premium/sum-assured conditions (normally 10%, 15% in specified disability/disease cases), death-receipt exceptions, and exclusions including Keyman policies and section 127(4). Qualifying IFSC Insurance Office policies issued from 1 April 2025 have an exception to the aggregate premium conditions.

**Provision / locator:** Section 11, Schedule II, Table item 2, column C and Note 1; legacy 10(10D).

## tax_sec_80ccd2_private

**Result:** 14%

Employer NPS deduction ceiling for non-government employees in the New Regime. 14% of defined salary where section 202 applies; otherwise the non-government ceiling is 10%. Salary includes qualifying DA but excludes other allowances/perquisites. This is a deduction ceiling, not an obligation for an employer to match contributions.

**Provision / locator:** Section 124(1)(b), (2), (13)(b), and section 202; legacy 80CCD(2).

## tax_sgb_secondary_rule

**Result:** SGB redemption exclusion requires an individual to hold from original issue until maturity

Enacted original-issue-to-maturity condition for an individual’s SGB redemption exclusion. An individual must hold the RBI-issued SGB from original issue until maturity. Secondary purchasers do not meet this exclusion. Sales, other redemptions and coupon interest need their own tax treatment; do not assume every transaction attracts 12.5%.

**Provision / locator:** Section 70(1)(x), substituted by Finance Act, 2026 from 1 April 2026.

## tax_sec_56_2_xii_reit_repayment

**Result:** Section 92(2)(k) (legacy 56(2)(xii))

Specified business-trust distributions use a cumulative statutory calculation. Taxable specified sum is max(A − B − C, 0): A is cumulative qualifying distributions to current/prior holders; B is unit issue price, not secondary purchase cost; C is sums already taxed under this clause. Excluded pass-through components and related cost-basis rules must be considered. Not every debt repayment is immediately taxable.

**Provision / locator:** Section 92(2)(k), read with section 223(4); legacy 56(2)(xii).

- [Income-tax Act, 2025, as amended by Finance Act, 2026](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/income_tax_act_2025_as_amended_by_fa_act_2026-pdf#page=160) — Section 92(2)(k), read with section 223(4); legacy 56(2)(xii); PDF page 160

## sebi_brokerage_cap_cash

**Result:** 6bps

Brokerage allowance outside the base expense ratio for cash trades. 6 bps cash / 2 bps derivatives of trade value may be charged outside BER. Excess brokerage falls within BER. Transaction costs and statutory levies are separate; these are not caps on every trading cost or the entire TER.

**Provision / locator:** SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 2026: regulations 66(9)–(10), 67.
