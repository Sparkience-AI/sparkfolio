# Support and corrections

Search [existing issues](https://github.com/Sparkience-AI/sparkfolio/issues) before opening a report. Support is best effort; Sparkfolio does not provide personal investment, insurance, legal or tax advice.

- For a factual correction, identify the section or claim ID, quote the disputed wording, and link the primary source with its effective date and passage. Explain the proposed correction without sharing personal financial information.
- For a bug, include the page URL, browser/device, expected and observed behavior, and safe steps to reproduce. Use invented answers and amounts in screenshots.
- For accessibility feedback, describe the task, browser, assistive technology and where progress became difficult.
- For feature proposals, explain the reader's need and how the change fits a static, private educational resource.

Never post account identifiers, balances, medical details, credentials or sensitive logs. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not an ordinary public bug report.
